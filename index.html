The room

Named for the air
it lets a voice keep.

Thoáng Acoustic sits one floor above Quán Sứ street, tucked into Hà Nội's Old Quarter. The name is Vietnamese for airy, open — the idea a room can be small and still let a song breathe.

No amplifiers pushing past what the room needs. Just voices, strings, and a bar that knows how to stay out of the way until you want another round.

What to expect

Three things, done well.

01

Live, every night

Acoustic sets from 5PM to 11PM, seven nights a week. Vocals and instruments mixed to be heard, not overpowered.

02

Cocktails, unhurried

A short, considered drinks list built for a room where conversation matters as much as the pour.

03

Something to share

Cold cut platters and light bites, meant for a table that's settling in rather than passing through.
